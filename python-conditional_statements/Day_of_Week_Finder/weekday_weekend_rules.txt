Day of Week Finder — Classification Rules
==========================================

Input:
- A number from 1 to 7, OR
- A day name (Monday through Sunday)

Number → Day Mapping:
1 -> Monday
2 -> Tuesday
3 -> Wednesday
4 -> Thursday
5 -> Friday
6 -> Saturday
7 -> Sunday

Weekday vs Weekend Rule:
- Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday -> Weekday
- Saturday, Sunday -> Weekend

Invalid Input Handling:
- Numbers outside the range 1-7 -> "Invalid number. Enter a valid number between 1-7"
- Day names that don't match Monday-Sunday -> "Invalid day name. Enter a valid day (Monday-Sunday)"
- In both invalid cases, category is not applicable (N/A) and no day number/name is shown.
