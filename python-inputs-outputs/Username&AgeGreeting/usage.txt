========================================
Username & Age Greeting - Usage Example
========================================

How to run:
    python greeting.py

----------------------------------------
Example Run 1
----------------------------------------
========================================
Welcome to the Greeting Generator!
========================================
Enter your name: Alice
Enter your age: 25
========================================
Hello Alice! Next year you'll be 26 years old.
========================================

----------------------------------------
Example Run 2
----------------------------------------
========================================
Welcome to the Greeting Generator!
========================================
Enter your name: Rahul
Enter your age: 30
========================================
Hello Rahul! Next year you'll be 31 years old.
========================================

Notes:
- Age must be entered as a whole number (e.g. 25, not 25.5 or "twenty-five").
- Name and age are collected via input() and converted using int() where needed.
